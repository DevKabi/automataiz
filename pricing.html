Pricing & Subscription FAQs

Can I upgrade anytime?
Yes! You can instantly upgrade from Startup to Growth or Enterprise directly from your admin settings. Any remaining prorated balance on your current plan will be credited automatically.
Can I downgrade later?
Yes, you can downgrade your plan at the end of your billing cycle. Your data, CRM contacts, and historical workflows will remain safely preserved in read-only mode.
Do you offer custom pricing?
Yes! For large organizations, healthcare networks with HIPAA requirements, or agencies with 50+ client sub-accounts, our Enterprise team creates custom packages with dedicated VPC hosting and tailored SLA agreements.
Is GST applicable?
For Indian businesses paying in INR, an 18% GST is applicable at checkout. You can enter your GSTIN during billing to claim full input tax credit (ITC) on all automated invoices.
Do you offer onboarding support?
Yes! Growth and Enterprise plans include dedicated white-glove onboarding and free data migration assistance from Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho, or CSV spreadsheets.
Can I pay yearly?
Yes! Annual billing gives you massive savings of up to 58% (saving ₹55,989 on Startup and ₹49,989 on Growth). Annual invoices can be paid via Corporate Card, Net Banking, or Wire Transfer.
What payment methods are supported in India?
We support all Indian payment methods including UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), Net Banking across 50+ banks, Credit/Debit Cards, Corporate Cards, and Wire Transfers via Razorpay and Stripe with instant activation.
Do you provide GST-compliant tax invoices?
Yes! All Indian payments are billed natively in INR (₹) and include valid 18% GST tax invoices with your business GSTIN number (AWAZME CONSULTING SERVICE GSTIN: 21BIIPK9304G1ZI) so you can claim 100% input tax credit (ITC).
