Common Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Clear answers about migrating your data, compliance, WhatsApp integration, and deployment.

How does Automataiz replace 10+ different SaaS tools?
Automataiz is engineered on a single unified core database architecture. Instead of buying a separate CRM (like HubSpot), a separate WhatsApp gateway, an email automation tool, an invoicing app, and Zapier to connect them, all 8 modules run natively inside Automataiz. When a lead takes an action anywhere, the whole system knows instantly.
Is Automataiz an official Meta WhatsApp Cloud API partner?
Yes. We integrate directly with the official Meta WhatsApp Cloud API. You get green checkmark verification support, zero risk of number bans, high throughput rate limits, and full support for interactive buttons, catalogs, and media templates.
How difficult is it to migrate our existing data?
We provide automated 1-click migration connectors for Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho, Pipedrive, and Stripe. Our Enterprise team also provides a dedicated Solutions Architect who handles full white-glove data cleansing and pipeline transfer in under 48 hours.
What are the security and compliance certifications?
Automataiz maintains SOC2 Type II, ISO 27001, GDPR, and HIPAA compliance. All customer data is protected with AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit. We also offer dedicated Single-Tenant VPC deployments for regulated enterprise customers.
Can we train the AI assistants on our own proprietary documents?
Yes. You can upload internal PDFs, standard operating procedures (SOPs), knowledge bases, and product sheets into your private RAG vector vault. Your AI agents will strictly adhere to your business rules and will never hallucinate or leak data outside your organization.
